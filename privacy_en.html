Lorebind Privacy Policy

App: Lorebind (the "App") · Effective: June 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Yuichiro Suzuki (the "Provider") sets out the following Privacy Policy regarding the handling of user information in the App.

1. Basic Policy

The App stores the data you create (worlds, characters, timelines, images, etc.) on your device. If you use iCloud sync, it is also stored in your own iCloud (an area on Apple's servers that only you can use). Other than this, the App never automatically sends your data to the Provider's servers or any third party, and the Provider does not collect it. (For sharing and exporting that you do yourself, see Section 7.)

2. Information We Collect

The Provider does not collect any personal information through the App, such as your name, contact details, location, or contacts. We do not perform usage analytics or behavioral tracking, and we do not show ads.

3. Your Created Data

All text and images you create, enter, or attach in the App are stored only on your device, in your own iCloud (if you use sync), and in files you choose to export yourself. When you attach images from Photos, the App imports only the images you select and does not ask for access to your whole photo library. The Provider cannot view or obtain this content.

4. iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, data is sent and received between your own devices via Apple's servers only when you tap a button on the App's Settings screen. This storage and transfer are handled by Apple's iCloud, and Apple's privacy policy applies. The Provider does not receive this content.

5. In-App Purchases

All purchases, billing, renewals, and cancellations of the paid plan (Lorebind Plus) are handled through Apple's App Store. Payment and credit card information is managed by Apple; the Provider does not receive it. Please see Apple's privacy policy for details.

6. Crash Records

If the App crashes, it stores the number, time, and type of the crash reports it receives from iOS. This record is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Provider. Whether diagnostic information is shared with Apple depends on your device settings.

7. Sharing and Exporting

The App passes data only to the destination you choose (such as Photos, Files, or other apps), and only when you share or export. When saving to Photos, the App asks only for permission to add photos.

8. Disclosure / External Transmission

The Provider does not provide your information to third parties. The App does not embed any external analytics or advertising services.

9. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information, it can be used safely by people of any age.

10. Contact

11. Amendments

This policy may be amended as needed. Significant changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
